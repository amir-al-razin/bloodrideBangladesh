## 1. Workflow agreed

The team's workflow now drives the design:
- QR poster in the hospital → form + doctor's slip → automatic slip check.
- Search wave → web push and Telegram alerts → live progress board.
- The donor accepts and may share their number → volunteer rider → bKash payment → donation, rest, ride home.
- Focus: students and campus clusters of volunteers.

## 2. Database changes (14 → 16 entities)

- **New CAMPUS table.** APP_USER has `campus_id`.
- **Guest accounts.** Patients sign in, or continue as a guest in an emergency. APP_USER has `account_type` (guest / member / admin); `password_hash` is null for guests. BLOOD_REQUEST has `requester_id`, `needed_by` and `tracking_token` (private progress link).
- **Live progress.** DONOR_ALERT has `seen_at`, so the board shows alerted / seen / declined / accepted.
- **Phone sharing.** MISSION has `phone_shared` (the donor's choice when accepting).
- **One escort both ways.** The rider moved from TRIP to MISSION (`rider_id`, "escorts"). RIDER has `rider_type` (volunteer / assigned).
- **Fares shown before booking.** New **FARE_RATE** table (base, per km, per minute, minimum, effective_from). TRIP has `rate_id`, `est_km`, `est_min`, `quoted_fare_bdt`, `actual_km`, `final_fare_bdt`.
- **Fuel cost removed.** The rider no longer types in a fuel cost.
- **Payments.** PAYMENT has `bkash_txn_id`; the payer is the patient. PAYOUT_ENTRY kind is fare / adjustment.
- **Self-reported donations.** DONATION with no mission = self-reported ("I donated elsewhere"), which starts the 90-day wait.
- New function **`quote_fare`** and new trigger **`trg_return_after_rest`**: 6 procedures, 4 functions, 7 triggers.
- The ERD was re-laid so no lines or labels overlap.

## 3. Ride plan (from research)

- One student volunteer with a motorbike takes the donor to the hospital, waits with them, and brings them home.
- Fare predicted from the route, like Pathao/Uber. **Starting rates: Tk 25 base + Tk 15/km + Tk 0.50/min, minimum Tk 50** (Pathao's last published bike rates, 2022). Example: 8.4 km, 32 min = Tk 167.
- The patient confirms the fare first; only then are riders alerted. First to accept wins, same campus first.
- The ride home starts only after the 20-minute rest, with a fresh quote. The final fare equals the quote unless the route is more than 20% longer.
- The patient pays each leg by bKash; the app records the transaction ID (sandbox later). **The donor pays nothing and is never paid** (WHO/IFRC: covering travel keeps the donation voluntary).
- No waiting fee and no CNG fallback for now. A club-funded charity option for poor patients comes later.
- Research covered: Pathao + Hamro LifeBank (Nepal), Uber India donor rides, Lyft/Waymo with the Red Cross, UK Blood Bikes, Amra Roktosandhani (Bangladesh), a Brazilian ride-discount study (+97.4% donations), WHO/IFRC rules, BRTA ride-sharing rules, Bangladesh fuel prices. Sources are listed in `summary.md` section 7.
